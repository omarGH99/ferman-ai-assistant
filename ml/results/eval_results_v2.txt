Two things these numbers say that the headline 0.83 does not:

1. Sorani is the one language v2 did not improve, and it is the weakest of the
   four at 0.6973 — roughly one intent in three wrong. This matches the live
   failure seen during testing (`هەولێر چیە` classified as news_query at 0.823
   confidence: wrong, and confident enough to sail past both the confidence
   floor and the margin check). Sorani and Badini have 370 test rows each
   against English and Arabic's 1954, so they are both undertrained and
   measured on a fifth of the evidence.

2. Arabic slot extraction is unmeasured and effectively untrained — 304
   annotated rows out of 11,097, none in test. Arabic intent classification is
   fine (0.8234); it is the *slot* half that is hollow. An Arabic reminder can
   therefore be routed to the right intent and still lose its date and time.
   Worth an English/Arabic side-by-side check by hand before leaning on it.
